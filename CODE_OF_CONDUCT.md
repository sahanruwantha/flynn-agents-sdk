# Community expectations

Treat participants respectfully. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and sharing someone's private
information without consent are not welcome in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban repeated
or severe violations. Enforcement should consider context and be proportionate.
To report a concern privately, contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/sahanruwantha) and request a private channel.
Do not disclose another participant's personal information in public issues.

These expectations cover issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained
by this project. They apply equally to contributors and maintainers.
